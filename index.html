Our way of training

High standards.
No big egos.

Wolves in sheep’s clothing. A nonprofit club with serious ability, where getting better matters more than looking tough. Compete if you want to. You don’t have to.

Learn by doing. Look after each other.

No pad-work routines or push-up circuits. We build skills through live training games, positional sparring and technical rounds with a specific task to solve. You learn timing, decisions and what works against a partner who is responding.

That only works with trust. Challenge each other, control the intensity and help your training partners improve. Skills, confidence, enjoyment—and a reason to keep coming back.

The Wolves systemBronze, Silver & Gold

No paid grading days

The Wolf
standard.

Created by founder Vinny Connolly, the Bronze, Silver and Gold Wolves system recognises progress across the complete sport—not belts or time served. The coaching team agrees when a level has been earned. There are no paid grading days, and no pressure to compete.

B
Bronze Wolf

Fundamentals that hold up.

A dependable base across striking, wrestling and grappling, with the ranges beginning to connect.

S
Silver Wolf

Amateur-ready MMA.

Technical depth, composure and a complete MMA skill set suited to the amateur circuit.

Gold Wolf

Professional standard.

A complete, tested game capable of operating at professional level.
