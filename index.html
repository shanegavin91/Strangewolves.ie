THE CLUB

Not a fitness franchise.
A Galway MMA room.

Strange Wolves is a non-profit club with volunteer coaches. The culture has always been technical, self-motivated and laid back: take the rounds seriously, keep learning, and don’t take yourself too seriously.

“Pressure test your skills against fully resisting opponents. See what works and discard what doesn’t.”
Vinny Connolly Founder · Head CoachMarcus Fields Co-Head Coach · Operations

THE WOLF SYSTEM

Bronze. Silver. Gold.

Vinny’s progression system looks at the whole MMA game rather than pretending striking, wrestling and ground work live in separate boxes.
