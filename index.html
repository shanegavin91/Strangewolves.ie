STRANGE WOLVES MMA

Serious training.
No ego required.

Founded by Vinny Connolly, Strange Wolves grew around a simple idea: train MMA as MMA — pressure-tested, technical and connected across every range.

The club remains non-profit and volunteer coached. People come to learn, improve and contribute to the room.

OUR STORY

One of Galway’s long-standing MMA teams.

LATE 2000s

The early pack

Strange Wolves was already representing Galway on the Irish MMA scene by 2009, with the club rooted in Creaven House in the Claddagh during the sport’s early growth locally.

THE SYSTEM

MMA first

Vinny developed the club curriculum around the skills that transfer across a full MMA fight: striking, wrestling and ground work rather than treating them as separate sports.

TODAY

Still Strange

The club remains volunteer coached and non-profit, with Vinny still leading as head coach and Marcus Fields now running day-to-day operations alongside him as co-head coach.

THE WOLF SYSTEM

Progress across every range.

Strange Wolves uses its own MMA progression framework. Rather than measuring only one discipline, the system looks at striking, wrestling and ground competence together.

THE CULTURE

Hard rounds.
Good people.

That balance has followed the club for years: take the training seriously without taking yourself too seriously. The result is a technical room with a strong social side and a real sense of team.

The club has also used that community to support its own, including fundraising events organised around the Galway combat-sports community.
