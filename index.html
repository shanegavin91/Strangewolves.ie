THE CLUB

Long-standing Galway MMA.
Still done the same way.

Strange Wolves has been part of Galway’s MMA scene since the late 2000s. The club was founded by Vinny Connolly and remains non-profit and volunteer coached.

The philosophy is simple: pressure-test technique, connect striking, wrestling and ground work, and keep what works.
